Privacy Policy

A-Live Memories

Effective date: 9 August 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how A-Live Memories handles information when you use the application.

A-Live Memories is designed to work without creating an account. The developer does not operate a server that collects your gameplay, memory selections, reports, or saved images.

Information Stored on Your Device

A-Live Memories stores the following information locally on your device so that your progress can continue between sessions:

  • Game progress, score, streak, and current board layout
  • Collected memories and question selections
  • Your generated collection report
  • Music, volume, and other app preferences
  • Premium entitlement status provided by Apple StoreKit

This information is used only to operate the app and personalise your in-app experience. It is not uploaded to the developer.

Personalised Collection Report

The Story of Your Collection is generated from the choices you make while using the app. The report is created locally on your device. Your choices and report are not sent to the developer or used for advertising, tracking, credit decisions, employment decisions, or other decisions affecting your legal rights.

Photos Permission

A-Live Memories requests permission to add images to your Photos library only when you choose to save a memory card or background image.

The app does not access or upload your existing photos. Images saved to Photos remain under your control and can be deleted using the Photos app.

In-App Purchases

Premium purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Apple may process purchase and transaction information according to its own privacy policy. You can read Apple’s privacy policy at apple.com/legal/privacy .

Information We Do Not Collect

A-Live Memories does not include developer-operated:

  • User accounts or login systems
  • Advertising or cross-app tracking
  • Analytics or behavioural profiling
  • Location collection
  • Contact-list collection
  • Cloud storage of gameplay or memory selections

Data Retention and Deletion

Gameplay information remains on your device until you reset the game or delete the application. You can reset your progress using the Restart Game option in Settings.

Deleting the app removes its locally stored information. Images previously saved to Photos must be deleted separately from the Photos app. Apple may retain purchase records as required to operate the App Store and comply with legal obligations.

Children’s Privacy

A-Live Memories does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require users to provide their name, email address, location, or other identifying information.

Website Hosting

This privacy-policy page may be hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical information when you visit the page, according to the GitHub Privacy Statement .

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or information practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.
