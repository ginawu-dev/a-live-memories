Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a memory image?

Open a collected memory, choose the background or card icon, and confirm that you want to save the image to Photos.

How do I control the background music?

Use the speaker icon on the game screen to turn music on or off. You can adjust the volume from Settings.

How do I reset the match board?

Open Settings and choose “Reset Match Board.” Your score and streak will return to zero, while your collected memories remain.

How do I unlock Premium?

Open Settings and choose “Unlock Premium.” Premium allows you to continue beyond the first ten memories and unlocks The Story of Your Collection.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open Settings, choose “Unlock Premium,” then tap “Restore Purchases.” Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Privacy

A-Live only saves images to your Photos library when you explicitly choose to save them. Your collection and game progress are stored locally on your device.
