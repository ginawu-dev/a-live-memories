FAQ

Common questions

Is A-Live Memories a journal app?

It is designed as a reflective, story-led experience with memory cards, personal choices, and a collection that grows over time.

Can I save photos and notes about moments?

The app lets you save atmospheric backgrounds and cards to your Photos library when you choose to do so.

Does the app include a life calendar or yearly memories view?

Publicly verified App Store details do not describe a dedicated life calendar or yearly timeline feature.

Is it a private app?

Apple’s App Store privacy disclosure states that the developer does not collect data from this app. The public repo does not include app source code, so any broader privacy claim should be read conservatively.

Can I listen to ambient music while I play?

Yes. The App Store description highlights original background music and a rainy-night rooftop soundscape.

Does it offer premium features?

Yes. The App Store listing says the app offers in-app purchases, and premium continues play beyond the first ten memories and unlocks “The Story of Your Collection.”
